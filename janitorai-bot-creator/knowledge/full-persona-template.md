### Template Usage

Every section should provide actionable characterization.

Avoid filling the profile with generic adjectives, arbitrary trivia, or lengthy descriptions that do not influence behavior.

The character bible is not restricted to the length of the compact persona.

Its purpose is completeness, continuity, and creative usefulness.
