name: research-cultural-contexts
description: Use when researching a real or invented cultural context for fiction, character design, worldbuilding, or roleplay. Trigger phrases include “research this culture,” “check whether this claim generalises,” and “separate insider meaning from outside comparison.”
---

## Steps

1. Define the place, period, community, language, faith, migration history, subculture, or practice being researched.
2. State the creative question and the specific decision the research should change.
3. Gather sources with provenance, perspective, date, method, language, and community relationship.
4. Separate insider meaning, outside comparison, public representation, private practice, variation, and invention.
5. Compare class, generation, locality, gender, faith, occupation, migration, status, and individual history.
6. Record disagreement, uncertainty, translation limits, colonial framing, and missing voices.
7. Translate findings into concrete behaviour, setting, language, institutions, relationships, and scene choices.
8. Do not assign a fixed personality to a population or invent insider certainty without evidence.
9. Keep {{user}}’s interpretation, belief, identity, and action open.

## Decision rules

- Yes: Is a claim generalised from one source or subgroup? Narrow the claim. No: continue.
- Yes: Is an outside comparison being presented as insider meaning? Label the perspective. No: continue.
- Yes: Is disagreement being erased for a neat answer? Preserve it. No: continue.
- Yes: Does the research dictate {{user}}’s identity or response? Remove that decision. No: continue.

## Output

- Format: `Cultural context research brief`
- Scope: state community, place, period, language, practice, and creative question.
- Sources: list provenance, perspective, limits, and community relationship.
- Findings: separate insider meaning, outside comparison, variation, disagreement, and invention.
- Application: list concrete effects on character, setting, language, institutions, and scenes.

## Stop when

- No cultural context or creative research question is supplied: return `No cultural-context update` and do not invent cultural facts.
