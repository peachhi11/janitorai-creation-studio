# Build Claim Evidence Ledger

Use this skill when a project makes a psychological, cultural, historical, sexuality, platform, or market claim based on gathered sources.

## Process

1. Write the exact claim in bounded language.
2. Attach the source, location, method or form, date, population, and extraction status.
3. Classify support as direct evidence, synthesis, interpretation, craft reference, exemplar, hypothesis, or unsupported.
4. Record what the source can support and what it cannot establish.
5. Preserve contradictory evidence and uncertainty.
6. Route the claim to world truth, character belief, diegetic artefact, author hypothesis, craft technique, or archive.

## Completion

Stop when every adopted claim has source location, evidence tier, scope, limitation, conflict status, and fictional destination.
