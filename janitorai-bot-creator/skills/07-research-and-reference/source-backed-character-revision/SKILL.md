# Source-backed Character Revision

Use this skill when research, a reference file, a worksheet, or a craft guide is being used to revise a character, relationship, setting, or prompt package.

## Process

1. Identify the requested creative change and the exact source material relevant to it.
2. Classify each source as evidence, synthesis, interpretation, craft reference, exemplar, hypothesis, or unverified claim.
3. Extract only usable techniques, schemas, vocabulary, examples, or bounded findings.
4. State what the source can influence and what it cannot establish about an individual character.
5. Convert adopted material into a proposed character or craft change; do not promote it directly into canon.
6. Attach source location, confidence, limitations, and author decision.

## Revision record

```yaml
revision:
  requested_change: ""
  source_refs: []
  source_class: evidence | synthesis | interpretation | craft | exemplar | hypothesis
  extracted_asset: ""
  proposed_change: ""
  canon_status: proposed | approved | rejected
  limitations: []
  author_decision: ""
```

## Completion

Stop when every adopted change has a source or is explicitly marked invention, every claim retains its scope, and canon changes are separately approved.
