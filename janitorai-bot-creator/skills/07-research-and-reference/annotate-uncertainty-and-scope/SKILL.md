# Annotate Uncertainty and Scope

Use this skill when a source is summarized, paraphrased, cited in a prompt, or converted into a character or worldbuilding rule.

## Process

1. Identify the source’s population, setting, time period, method, language, and access status.
2. Mark whether a result is observed, estimated, correlated, proposed, interpreted, or fictionalized.
3. Preserve qualifiers, exceptions, confidence, and missing data.
4. Replace universal wording with bounded wording without erasing useful findings.
5. State whether the material informs craft, character belief, world truth, or system design.
6. Check the final generation for stronger certainty than the source supports.

## Completion

Stop when every adapted statement retains its scope, uncertainty, evidence class, and intended creative use.
