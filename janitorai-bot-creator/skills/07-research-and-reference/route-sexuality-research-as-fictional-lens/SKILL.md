# Route Sexuality Research as Fictional Lens

Use this skill when sexuality, arousal, orgasm, anatomy, communication, attraction, or intimacy research is retrieved for fictional character or scene design.

## Process

1. Classify the source as primary research, review, theory, anatomy reference, craft guide, erotic exemplar, personal account, tactic material, or unverified claim.
2. Record population, context, method, limitations, date, and whether the material actually supports the requested claim.
3. Extract usable vocabulary, variation, scene mechanics, historical context, or character-belief material.
4. Keep bodies, desire, arousal, orgasm, consent, identity, relationship meaning, and behavior as separate dimensions.
5. Route uncertainty and disagreement into character belief, diegetic artefact, author hypothesis, or explicit fiction rather than authorial law.
6. Apply adult-character, agency, consent, privacy, and platform constraints at the output stage.

## Completion

Stop when every adopted detail has a bounded source use, limitation, fictional routing, and no unsupported claim has been presented as a universal sexual rule.
