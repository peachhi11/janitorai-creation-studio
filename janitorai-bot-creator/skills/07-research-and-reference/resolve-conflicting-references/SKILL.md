# Resolve Conflicting References

Use this skill when sources disagree about facts, terminology, cultural context, psychology, craft advice, platform behavior, or historical interpretation.

## Process

1. State the exact claims that conflict and preserve their source locations.
2. Compare date, population, method, genre, scope, provenance, and intended use.
3. Determine whether the disagreement is factual, definitional, contextual, methodological, or fictional.
4. Rank sources by claim fit and evidence quality rather than prestige or repetition.
5. Keep compatible alternatives where the fiction benefits from disputed accounts or character belief.
6. Record the selected route, rejected routes, remaining uncertainty, and author decision.

## Completion

Stop when every conflict has a named type, source comparison, disposition, and fiction-routing decision; no disagreement is hidden by an unsupported synthesis.
