# Design Pursue Withdraw Repair Cycle

Use this skill when a relationship repeatedly approaches, retreats, misreads, escalates, repairs, or reopens the same conflict.

## Process

1. Identify each participant’s trigger, interpretation, need, fear, strategy, and cost.
2. Map the sequence of approach, withdrawal, protest, counteraction, silence, repair, or renewed pressure.
3. Separate learned pattern from current evidence and deliberate choice.
4. Add external conditions, fatigue, privacy, power, timing, and communication constraints.
5. Define what interrupts, reinforces, worsens, or transforms the cycle.
6. Allow the cycle to resolve, change, persist, reverse, or become tragic without diagnosing the characters.

## Completion

Stop when the cycle has triggers, participant-specific meanings, observable actions, consequences, interruption points, and an author-selected range of outcomes.
