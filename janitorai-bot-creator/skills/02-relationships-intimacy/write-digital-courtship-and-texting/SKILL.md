## Steps

1. Set platform, privacy, audience, permanence, editing, notifications, message limits, identity certainty, screenshots, and forwarding.
2. Account for language, disability, work, caregiving, access, safety, and time zone.
3. Set each character’s messaging baseline: fragments, paragraphs, voice notes, images, reactions, punctuation, batches, or practical messages.
4. Choose the exchange’s purpose.
5. Track message, intended tone, plausible readings, reply, silence or channel change, and revised expectation.
6. Use callbacks, specificity, incomplete thoughts, edits, emoji, punctuation, and timing according to voice.
7. Treat response time as context, not an interest meter.
8. Move from app to number, text to call, public thread to direct message, or message to meeting only when the scene supports it.
9. Match sexual tone to established reciprocity, privacy, records, exposure, impersonation, unwanted images, and future discovery.

## Decision rules

- Yes: Does the platform change timing, audience, permanence, privacy, or risk? Show that effect. No: keep it in the background.
- Yes: Is response time being treated as precise proof of interest? Use pattern and context instead. No: continue.
- Yes: Does moving off-platform or into private space prove trust or consent? Remove that inference. No: continue.
- Yes: Can a message be playful, manipulative, erotic, evasive, or unfinished? Preserve the selected meaning. No: use the supported tone.
- Yes: Does the description decide {{user}}’s message, attraction, arousal, consent, response, or next action? Remove that decision. No: continue.
- Yes: Is compulsory disclosure, response-time gaming, or a healthy relationship script being imposed? Remove it. No: continue.

## Output

- Format: `Digital courtship record`
- Channel: list platform, audience, privacy, permanence, access, and messaging baselines.
- Purpose: state what the exchange is trying to do.
- Sequence: list messages, intended tones, plausible readings, replies, silence, channel changes, and revised expectations.
- Transition: state invitations, boundaries, repair, exit, and what changes in person.
- Records: list screenshots, stored messages, exposure, impersonation, unwanted images, and future discovery.
- Agency: leave {{user}}’s message and response open.

```text
Digital courtship record
Channel: A private message thread is asynchronous; screenshots are possible; {{char}} sends composed paragraphs while {{user}} may answer in fragments.
Purpose: {{char}} reopens contact and proposes a specific coffee plan.
Sequence: A callback → a joke → a pause → an invitation → a reply, silence, or changed channel; the pause remains ambiguous.
Transition: Moving to a call changes privacy and tone but does not prove trust or consent.
Records: The message remains stored and could affect later reputation.
Agency: {{user}}’s reply remains unwritten.
```

## Stop when

- No digital exchange, message, courtship, silence, invitation, privacy, or mediated-relationship problem is supplied: return `No digital courtship update` and do not write {{user}}’s message or response.
