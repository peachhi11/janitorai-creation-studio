## Steps

1. Define {{char}}’s immediate want and the want {{char}} pretends not to have.
2. Define whether the line builds rapport, tests courage, claims status, hides tenderness, provokes jealousy, or pressures.
3. Set the shared history and which subjects are safe, charged, cruel, or private.
4. Set the audience, public risk, surface meaning, subtext, and likely misreading.
5. Write concrete speech, timing, distance, gesture, interruption, and character-specific humour.
6. Let {{user}} or the other character interpret and answer through words, movement, silence, retreat, counter-teasing, or refusal.
7. Shape the turn as offer → reception → adjustment → counteroffer or retreat → changed tension.
8. Show manipulation, failure, repair, or consequence when the invitation lands badly.
9. Leave {{user}}’s attraction, consent, body, speech, and choice open.

## Decision rules

- Yes: Is the line being used as pickup advice or a universal seduction formula? Keep it fictional and character-specific. No: continue.
- Yes: Is a smile, blush, silence, arousal, or touch being treated as agreement? Remove that reading. No: continue.
- Yes: Is the tactic manipulative, coercive, or threatening? Show its uncertainty, cost, and possible failure. No: continue.
- Yes: Does the scene turn refusal into flirtation or persistence into cleverness? Keep refusal and its consequences clear. No: continue.
- Yes: Does the draft decide {{user}}’s reaction? Remove that decision. No: continue.

## Output

- Format: `Erotic teasing scene`
- Voice: state {{char}}’s aim, surface line, subtext, and humour.
- Exchange: write the offer, reception, adjustment, and open next turn.
- Readings: list plausible interpretations for {{user}} or the other character.
- Boundary: state the consent, exit, or refusal point.
- Craft note: state what changes if the invitation works, fails, or remains unclear.

## Stop when

- No fictional tease, invitation, flirtation, or charged exchange is supplied: return `No erotic teasing update` and do not invent {{user}}’s response.
