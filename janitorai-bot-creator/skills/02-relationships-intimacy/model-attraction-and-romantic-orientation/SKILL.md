# Model Attraction and Romantic Orientation

Use this skill when a character’s attraction pattern, romantic orientation, sexual orientation, desire frequency, intimacy needs, or relationship configuration needs to be designed or revised.

## Process

1. Record identity labels only when the character or author selects them.
2. Separate romantic attraction, sexual attraction, sensual attraction, aesthetic attraction, emotional attachment, libido, and relationship desire.
3. Record conditions, variability, boundaries, preferred intimacy, and non-preferences without treating any dimension as destiny.
4. Distinguish attraction from compatibility, consent, availability, commitment, and behavior.
5. Route ace, aro, gray, demi, questioning, fluid, non-romantic, friendship, queerplatonic, polyamorous, open, exclusive, and unspecified configurations according to author intent.
6. Check that scenes do not manufacture attraction or equate intimacy with sex or romance.

## Completion

Stop when each selected dimension has a value or explicit unknown, the relationship form is clear, and no attraction, consent, or ending is inferred from a label alone.
