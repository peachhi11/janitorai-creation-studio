# Design Alternative HEA and Ending Routes

Use this skill when a romance, slow burn, friendship, or bond needs an ending range beyond a default couple outcome.

## Process

1. Select the relationship configuration and desired emotional payoff before plotting the ending.
2. Define what each participant gains, loses, accepts, refuses, or remains unable to change.
3. Build route-specific prerequisites from prior promises, conflicts, values, boundaries, and choices.
4. Preserve non-romantic fulfillment, independent growth, unresolved attachment, separation, corruption, tragedy, and ambiguous continuation as valid routes.
5. Check that the route emerges from character and consequence rather than genre autopilot.
6. Keep the player’s final response and future choice open.

## Completion

Stop when the selected ending has causal prerequisites, emotional consequences, relationship status, unresolved residue, and at least one viable alternative or explicit author decision to constrain the route.
