# Design Ace, Aro, and Non-romantic Routes

Use this skill when attraction, romance, sex, partnership, intimacy, or fulfillment should follow an ace, aro, gray, demi, queerplatonic, friendship, or intentionally independent route.

## Process

1. Establish the character’s selected identity language, attraction pattern, intimacy needs, boundaries, and relationship goals.
2. Separate emotional closeness, sensuality, sexuality, romance, domesticity, commitment, companionship, and shared purpose.
3. Identify external assumptions or pressures that create conflict without making the identity itself a problem to solve.
4. Design milestones meaningful to the selected relationship form.
5. Allow desire, uncertainty, negotiation, grief, joy, change, and non-resolution without compulsory conversion to romance or sex.
6. Check that labels inform but do not determine the individual’s behavior or ending.

## Completion

Stop when the route has specific forms of intimacy and fulfillment, conflict sources, boundaries, milestones, and an ending that does not treat romance or sex as the default measure of success.
