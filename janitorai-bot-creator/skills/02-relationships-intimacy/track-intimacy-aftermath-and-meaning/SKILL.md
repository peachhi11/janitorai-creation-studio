# Track Intimacy Aftermath and Meaning

Use this skill after adult fictional intimacy, erotic tension, negotiated power, refusal, boundary crossing, or emotionally consequential touch.

## Process

1. Record what occurred, what was permitted, what was refused, and what remained ambiguous.
2. Separate bodily response, desire, consent, emotion, interpretation, obligation, and relationship meaning.
3. Record each participant’s knowledge, private reading, public behavior, and unresolved reaction.
4. Update trust, warmth, fear, resentment, desire, availability, commitment, power, and status only where supported.
5. Track aftercare, avoidance, repair, retaliation, secrecy, disclosure, or changed boundaries where genre and character require them.
6. Preserve the event and its competing meanings in history.

## Completion

Stop when the event has immediate and delayed consequences, separate participant interpretations, affected relationship dimensions, and no forced emotional or romantic conclusion.
