# Design Training and Distillation Boundaries

Use this skill when converting character cards, roleplay transcripts, evaluations, source excerpts, or prompt traces into training or distillation data.

## Process

1. Define the target capability, source population, license, privacy status, and retention policy.
2. Separate canon examples, fictional style examples, evaluation labels, user data, and retrieved research.
3. Remove or transform private, identifying, unauthorized, or unnecessary intimate data.
4. Label genre, role, speaker, knowledge scope, player ownership, consent state, and intended behavior.
5. Balance positive, negative, ambiguous, refusal, disagreement, and unresolved examples.
6. Test for memorization, stereotype amplification, sycophancy, persona collapse, and overfitting to one genre.

## Completion

Stop when every training item has provenance, authorization, labels, privacy disposition, target behavior, and exclusion rationale where omitted.
