# Convert Persona to Portable Bot

Use this skill when a user persona must become a standalone bot, character counterpart, campaign identity, or portable roleplay card.

## Process

1. Load permanent persona canon and mark identity, values, boundaries, history, preferences, and unresolved fields.
2. Select the target bot role, setting, relationship position, genre, and platform schema.
3. Adapt expression, goals, speech, scenario, and current knowledge without rewriting permanent canon.
4. Render full, compact, and platform-specific forms with explicit provenance and adaptation scope.
5. Validate player agency, knowledge limits, identity consistency, and target-card compatibility.
6. Preserve the original persona and record the conversion as a reversible derivative.

## Completion

Stop when the portable package is schema-valid, the adaptation layer is separable, permanent canon is unchanged, and the source persona and conversion revision are recoverable.
