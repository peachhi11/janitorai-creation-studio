# Preserve Provenance in Exports

Use this skill when a generated or revised artifact leaves the Studio or is handed to another agent, platform, or collaborator.

## Process

1. Record source references, extraction locations, prompt or preset, model/provider, revision identifier, and author decisions.
2. Separate provenance metadata from in-world character voice and portable content.
3. Mark generated, inferred, retrieved, approved, proposed, transformed, and user-supplied fields.
4. Preserve limitations, unsupported claims, omitted material, and adaptation scope.
5. Validate that export conversion did not drop boundaries, knowledge rules, or revision identity.
6. Provide a minimal provenance manifest alongside the portable artifact.

## Completion

Stop when every nontrivial generated or sourced component is traceable, portable content remains cleanly separated from metadata, and the artifact can be audited back to its approved revision.
