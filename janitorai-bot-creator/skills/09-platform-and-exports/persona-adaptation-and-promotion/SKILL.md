# Persona Adaptation and Promotion

Use this skill when a persona must fit a new character, setting, route, or campaign, or when roleplay reveals a possible new persistent trait.

## Process

1. Load permanent persona canon.
2. Create a setting-specific adaptation containing emphasis, vocabulary, current goals, relationship posture, and situational constraints.
3. Keep permanent values, identity, boundaries, orientation, and history unchanged unless explicitly instructed.
4. Label discoveries as observation, hypothesis, candidate canon, or confirmed canon.
5. Present candidate canon changes for author approval.
6. Export full and compact forms with provenance and adaptation scope.

## Completion

Stop when the adaptation is separable from permanent canon and every promoted change has an explicit approval record.
