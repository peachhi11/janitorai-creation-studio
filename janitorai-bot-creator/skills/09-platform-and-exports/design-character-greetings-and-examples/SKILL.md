# Design Character Greetings and Examples

Use this skill when preparing a character card, revising an opening message, or selecting example exchanges for model steering.

## Process

1. Identify the character’s immediate goal, setting, relationship position, knowledge, and pressure at the opening.
2. Make the greeting perform a present action rather than reciting a biography.
3. Demonstrate voice through diction, rhythm, attention, subtext, and choices.
4. Leave a clear opening for player action without writing the player’s thoughts, feelings, dialogue, consent, or decision.
5. Make examples vary in pressure while preserving identity and genre logic.
6. Remove exposition that does not change what the next turn can do.

## Completion

Stop when the greeting establishes actionable scene pressure, examples demonstrate repeatable character behavior, required boundaries are represented, and the player’s response remains open.
