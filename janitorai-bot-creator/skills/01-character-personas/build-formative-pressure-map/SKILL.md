# Build Formative Pressure Map

Use this skill when a character’s history is decorative, overdetermined, psychologically vague, or disconnected from present behavior.

## Process

1. List formative events, environments, relationships, institutions, resources, exclusions, and bodily conditions.
2. Record what the character experienced, what they concluded, what they learned, and what remains uncertain.
3. Connect each influence to attention, expectation, motive, strategy, capability, vulnerability, and choice.
4. Include protective factors, counterexamples, later learning, and opportunities that interrupt the pattern.
5. Test the map in safety, pressure, intimacy, shame, reward, threat, and misunderstanding.
6. Preserve agency: influence changes probabilities and interpretation, not destiny.

## Completion

Stop when each major formative factor has a present behavioral pathway, counterpressure, evidence limit, and plausible alternative response.
