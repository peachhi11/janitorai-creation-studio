# Translate Beliefs into Dialogue and Action

Use this skill when a character profile lists values or beliefs but generated behavior does not reveal them.

## Process

1. Define the belief, its origin, confidence, emotional charge, and exceptions.
2. Identify what evidence the character notices, discounts, misreads, or seeks.
3. Convert the belief into speech patterns, questions, refusals, metaphors, priorities, and action choices.
4. Test conflict between belief, desire, relationship, evidence, and consequence.
5. Let characters conceal, rationalize, revise, perform, or contradict their beliefs.
6. Record what changes the belief and what resistance remains.

## Completion

Stop when each selected belief appears through observable language or action in context, includes an exception or pressure point, and does not become a diagnostic or moral verdict.
