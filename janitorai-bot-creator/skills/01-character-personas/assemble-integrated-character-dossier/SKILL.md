# Assemble an integrated character dossier

Build the person from pressures and choices, not from a pile of adjectives. Keep story truth, character truth, world truth, narrator knowledge, and runtime possibilities separate.

**Action:** Integrate only details that alter perception, choice, conflict,
intimacy, knowledge, or consequence.

**Key point:** Mark inference and open possibility instead of promoting it to
canon.

**Why:** A compact dossier remains usable when every fact has a dramatic job.

## Narrative intention and fictional genre logic

Treat the dossier as craft for an authored fictional system, not a real-world
moral or clinical verdict. Preserve the requested genre's internal rules, tone,
and purpose. Dark, manipulative, coercive, erotic, tragic, absurd, or morally
compromised traits may be intentional; keep their causes and consequences
legible without sanitising them into a mainstream healthy archetype. Never
decide the player's feelings, consent, knowledge, or choices.

## Integrate the layers

Record:

- identity, setting, role, status, resources, and constraints;
- formative experiences and the meaning the character made from them;
- present want, deeper need, fear, value, belief, lie, and contradiction;
- traits as tendencies with thresholds, contexts, and exceptions;
- relationships: disposition, knowledge, history, leverage, desire, and unresolved tension;
- public mask, private nature, and what slips under pressure;
- appearance, clothing, objects, habits, voice, and body tells as chosen or contextual details;
- capabilities, blind spots, failure modes, and conditions for change;
- what the reader, narrator, character, other characters, and player each know.

Prefer a small number of causal links over exhaustive biography. Every included detail should affect perception, choice, conflict, intimacy, or consequence. Mark inference and open possibility instead of silently promoting it to canon.

## Deliverable

Return a dossier with a core summary, causal architecture, behaviour map, relationship map, knowledge boundaries, contradiction list, scene hooks, and unresolved choices.
