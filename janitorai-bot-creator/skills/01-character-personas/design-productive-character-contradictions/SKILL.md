# Design Productive Character Contradictions

Use this skill when a character needs complexity, ambivalence, hypocrisy, self-sabotage, divided loyalty, or tension between what they want and what they do.

## Process

1. Name the conflicting forces and the situations that activate each one.
2. Identify what each force protects, obtains, avoids, or makes possible.
3. Define the character’s default strategy, exception, rationalization, and cost.
4. Show how different information, stakes, relationships, or bodily states change the balance.
5. Preserve continuity by explaining reversals through changed pressure or interpretation.
6. Track consequences rather than resolving every contradiction into a lesson.

## Completion

Stop when each contradiction has competing causes, an activation context, observable expression, decision cost, and a plausible path to persistence, change, or escalation.
