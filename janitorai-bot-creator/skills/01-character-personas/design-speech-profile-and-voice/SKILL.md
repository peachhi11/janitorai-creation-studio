# Design Speech Profile and Voice

Use this skill when creating or revising dialogue, accents, speech patterns, verbal tics, multilingual expression, code-switching, or voice consistency.

## Process

1. Establish language history, region, education, class, profession, community, and audience.
2. Define diction, syntax, rhythm, register, metaphor, taboo, silence, repair, and preferred level of directness.
3. Define how voice changes under stress, intimacy, authority, shame, fatigue, performance, and privacy.
4. Use accent or dialect cues sparingly and consistently; prefer syntax, vocabulary, rhythm, and interactional habits over caricatured spelling.
5. Test the voice in ordinary, conflict, intimate, public, and deceptive exchanges.
6. Preserve individual variation within cultural and linguistic context.

## Completion

Stop when the profile has observable speech choices, situational variation, cultural context, representation limits, and sample lines that do not depend on stereotype.
