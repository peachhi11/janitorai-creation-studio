# Reconcile Post-scene Consequences

Use this skill after generating or authoring a scene that contains a meaningful event, reveal, decision, injury, promise, refusal, or change of access.

## Process

1. Compare scene entry and exit state.
2. Extract objective events, participant actions, witnessed facts, claims, beliefs, and unresolved interpretations.
3. Update only affected world, character, relationship, knowledge, object, reputation, and open-thread records.
4. Check dependencies, supersession, sequence, and coupled state.
5. Flag missing consequences, impossible knowledge, duplicated facts, and unsupported permanence.
6. Produce accepted commits, review-required proposals, and archived evidence.

## Completion

Stop when every material scene change has a ledger destination and evidence, every unresolved consequence remains visible, and no unsupported state has been committed.
