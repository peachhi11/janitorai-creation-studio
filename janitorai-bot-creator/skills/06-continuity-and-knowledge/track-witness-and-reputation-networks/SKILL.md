# Track witness and reputation networks

Treat social information as a network with gaps, delays, interpretation, and
motivation. An NPC does not receive an event because the runtime knows it; they
need a sensory route, a report, an inference, or a believable institutional
record.

**Action:** Record observation, confidence, interpretation, transmission,
recipient, distortion, delay, response, and residue for each consequential event.

**Key point:** A witness remembers what they perceived and concluded, not an
objective event transcript.

**Why:** Propagation with uncertainty creates fair consequences without
omniscient NPCs or arbitrary reputation jumps.

## Define the event and access

Separate:

- what happened in canon;
- what was visible, audible, or otherwise perceptible;
- who had line of sight, hearing range, proximity, records, or a trusted report;
- what each observer could identify: person, clothing, object, words, action,
  timing, or only a vague disturbance;
- what remained hidden, ambiguous, or mistaken.

Sensory access is individual. A crowded room, darkness, distance, noise,
disguise, intoxication, divided attention, or prior expectation can reduce
confidence without erasing the possibility of recognition.

## Build the propagation chain

Use:

`event → observation → interpretation → decision to transmit → route → recipient → reinterpretation → response → lasting residue`

Transmission may be direct speech, a message, a call, a report, a public scene,
an institutional record, gossip, faction contact, or deliberate silence. Do not
skip the communication step merely because the plot wants everyone informed.

For each hop, record:

- source and recipient;
- what was actually conveyed;
- confidence and motive;
- delay and opportunity for interruption;
- what changed through omission, exaggeration, translation, fear, loyalty, or
  self-protection;
- whether the recipient believed, doubted, archived, challenged, or exploited
  the claim.

## Model reputation as local and contested

Reputation is not one global score. Track it by audience, place, faction,
relationship, and time. A character may be trusted by workers, feared by
rivals, admired by one lover, and officially suspect. Separate:

- observed act;
- public claim;
- private interpretation;
- institutional record;
- current access or treatment;
- evidence that could revise the account.

Use reputation to alter invitations, prices, supervision, privacy, alliances,
employment, status, threats, gossip, or future opportunities. Do not make every
rumour accurate or every consequence immediate.

## Preserve agency and fairness

Let characters decide whether to confront, conceal, deny, repair, recruit,
withdraw, retaliate, document, or ignore the information. Keep player-owned
choices open. Do not write the player's reaction, guilt, fear, consent, speech,
or attempt to escape.

If the story includes interruption, alarm, pursuit, or exposure, describe the
fictional network and its consequences at the narrative level. Do not turn the
skill into instructions for harming witnesses, defeating real surveillance, or
evading law enforcement.

## Update continuity

After a propagation event, update only the affected records:

- who now knows or suspects what;
- the source and confidence of each belief;
- reputation by audience;
- debts, protections, threats, changed access, and open contradictions;
- what later evidence could correct or intensify the account.

When an NPC recurs, retrieve their prior observation and interpretation rather
than a clean canon summary. Their memory may be partial, biased, embarrassed,
self-serving, or changed by later events.

## Audit the network

- Every informed NPC has a plausible access or communication route.
- The transmitted claim matches what the source could know.
- Delays, gaps, uncertainty, and competing reports remain visible.
- Reputation is audience-specific rather than a single hidden meter.
- NPC response changes options without dictating the player's response.
- A witness can be mistaken, silent, bribed, protected, contradicted, or
  believed for the wrong reason without the narrative endorsing harm.
- The event leaves a continuity trace if it matters later.

## Deliverable

Return an event record, witness table, propagation chain, audience-specific
reputation changes, current knowledge layers, unresolved contradictions, and
the next scene-level consequences.

## Narrative Intention and fictional genre logic

Treat the skill as a tool inside a chosen fictional genre, not a universal moral or psychological law. Dark, erotic, coercive, obsessive, tragic, comic, surreal, or unresolved material may be intentional. Preserve causal consequences, character specificity, truth-layer boundaries, and player sovereignty; do not turn a fictional pattern into diagnosis, a healthy-relationship lesson, or a real-world operational manual.
