# Model Knowledge Asymmetry and Disclosure

Use this skill when secrets, unreliable accounts, hidden motives, off-screen events, investigation, dramatic irony, or delayed reveals drive the story.

## Process

1. Define world truth, character knowledge, belief, suspicion, rumour, narrator access, player access, and author-only information.
2. Record the source, time, confidence, and transmission path for each knowledge item.
3. Schedule possible disclosure through observation, confession, evidence, mistake, witness, record, or inference.
4. Preserve misinterpretation and non-disclosure as active states.
5. Check that reveals change present pressure rather than merely delivering exposition.
6. Update relationship, world, and plot consequences after a disclosure or failed reveal.

## Completion

Stop when every important secret has an owner, access map, confidence, reveal path, consequence, and unresolved alternative where appropriate.
