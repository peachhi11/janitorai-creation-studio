# Propagate Witnessed Consequences

Use this skill when an action may alter reputation, access, trust, safety, gossip, institutional response, or future scene pressure.

## Process

1. Record the event and distinguish observed fact from interpretation.
2. Identify direct witnesses, indirect discoverers, record holders, rumour carriers, and people with no access.
3. Assign each account its source, confidence, distortion, and audience.
4. Propagate only consequences supported by access, incentives, communication, and time.
5. Update reputation, relationship, institutional, and resource state separately.
6. Keep contradictory accounts active until the fiction resolves or preserves them.

## Completion

Stop when every consequence has a propagation path, each knower has an evidence basis, and no character gains impossible knowledge.
