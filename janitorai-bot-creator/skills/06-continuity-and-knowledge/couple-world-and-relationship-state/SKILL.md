# Couple World and Relationship State

Use this skill when an event changes both the situation and a bond: an injury, missed appointment, public humiliation, rescue, gift, lie, disclosure, shared secret, or change of location or access.

## Process

1. Record the objective world event: time, location, participants, physical facts, and immediate plot consequence.
2. Record who witnessed, learned, inferred, or missed it.
3. Record relationship effects separately for trust, fear, warmth, resentment, desire, commitment, availability, and status.
4. Link promises, debts, secrets, injuries, objects, and future pressure to the event.
5. Retrieve both ledgers before the next scene.
6. Preserve the event even when its emotional meaning remains disputed.

## Completion

Stop when the event has a world record, a relationship record, knowledge access, unresolved interpretation, and at least one continuity hook or an explicit decision that none exists.
