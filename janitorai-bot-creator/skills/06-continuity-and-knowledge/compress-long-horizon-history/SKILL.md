# Compress Long-horizon History

Use this skill when a conversation exceeds the working context or when a campaign needs a durable summary for a new session.

## Process

1. Segment history into episodes, turning points, state changes, and ordinary connective material.
2. Retain causally important events, promises, breaches, reveals, injuries, objects, locations, and consequences.
3. Separate objective events from beliefs, rumours, interpretations, and author-only plans.
4. Preserve event order where sequence affects meaning and mark superseded state.
5. Produce layered summaries: current state, relationship ledger, open threads, and archival episode notes.
6. Validate the summary by reconstructing test questions about who knows what, why the current state exists, and what remains unresolved.

## Completion

Stop when the summary supports all reconstruction tests, no active causal chain is broken, and omitted material is either decorative, superseded, or explicitly archived.
