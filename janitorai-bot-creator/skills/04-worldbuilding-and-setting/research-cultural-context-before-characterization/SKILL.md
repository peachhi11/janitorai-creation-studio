# Research Cultural Context Before Characterization

Use this skill when a character’s culture, language, identity, history, community, or social position needs more than generic reference knowledge.

## Process

1. Define the character’s specific place, time, generation, language, community, class, institutions, and lived position.
2. Gather varied sources including direct voices, history, scholarship, material culture, and contemporary context where available.
3. Record internal diversity, disagreement, change, power, exclusion, and ordinary practice.
4. Separate contextual influence from the individual character’s personality, choices, and contradictions.
5. Flag stereotypes, outsider assumptions, missing voices, translation limits, and contested claims.
6. Route findings into knowledge, setting, speech, relationships, and behavior with provenance.

## Completion

Stop when the characterization has specific context, internal variation, source limits, individual agency, and a review path for culturally sensitive choices.
