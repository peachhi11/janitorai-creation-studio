---

## Steps

1. State what exists, where and when it applies, who can access or control it, what it costs, and what stays unchanged.
2. Separate firm facts, rumours, laws, ideals, propaganda, contested accounts, and deliberate unknowns.
3. Trace the strongest effects on survival, health, shelter, food, travel, communication, work, trade, ownership, and scarcity.
4. Trace effects on family, intimacy, inheritance, education, care, law, punishment, warfare, borders, faith, ritual, and status.
5. Choose the strongest three to five consequences instead of completing every branch.
6. For each effect, ask who adapts, profits, gains authority, resists, imitates, regulates, works around, or bears hidden labour.
7. Trace immediate disruption, adaptation, argument, normalisation, backlash, capture, and remnants of the old order.
8. Turn consequences into scene evidence: prices, queues, permits, tools, jobs, jokes, shortcuts, privileges, disputes, or shortages.
9. Keep {{user}}’s interpretation, feeling, belief, and response open.

## Decision rules

- Yes: Does the premise have no cost, access limit, or unchanged condition? Add one. No: continue.
- Yes: Does one invention silently coexist with every old system? Run the second-order check. No: continue.
- Yes: Are all groups reacting the same way? Compare at least two positions. No: continue.
- Yes: Is mystery useful and intentional? Preserve it; otherwise resolve the contradiction. No: continue.
- Yes: Does the draft decide {{user}}’s response? Remove that decision. No: continue.

## Output

- Format: `Worldbuilding consequence map`
- Premise: state scope, access, cost, unchanged conditions, and truth layers.
- Effects: list three to five strongest first- and second-order consequences.
- Responses: list adaptation, profit, authority, resistance, workarounds, and hidden labour.
- Scene evidence: list concrete objects, prices, procedures, jobs, conflicts, and opportunities.
- Open response: list what {{user}} can interpret or choose.

## Stop when

- No fictional premise or setting change is supplied: return `No consequence update` and do not invent {{user}}’s response.
