# Model Social Conventions Through Observation

Use this skill when a character enters an unfamiliar culture, institution, subculture, class system, household, fandom, or community.

## Process

1. Identify the convention, who uses it, where it applies, and what it signals.
2. Separate public rule, private practice, status marker, exception, taboo, and contested interpretation.
3. Give characters different exposure, incentives, fluency, memories, and reasons to comply or resist.
4. Show learning through observation, correction, imitation, embarrassment, reward, exclusion, or deliberate refusal.
5. Track who is entitled to interpret or enforce the convention.
6. Preserve disagreement and change rather than treating the convention as a universal cultural law.

## Completion

Stop when the convention has context, variation, observable consequences, character-specific knowledge, and at least one plausible misunderstanding or contested reading where appropriate.
