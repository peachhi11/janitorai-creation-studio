# Design Lore Activation Policy

Use this skill when building lorebooks, setting retrieval, conditional context, or location- and character-sensitive world information.

## Process

1. Define each lore item’s topic, scope, authority, audience, trigger, and expiry or supersession rule.
2. Separate availability to the system, accessibility to the viewpoint, and explicit disclosure in the scene.
3. Use location, participant, time, object, faction, relationship, and query signals as activation conditions.
4. Bound insertion count, depth, token cost, and recursion.
5. Preserve uncertainty, competing accounts, and author-only material.
6. Test activated, inactive, conflicting, stale, and inaccessible lore cases.

## Completion

Stop when every lore item has trigger, scope, access rule, budget, provenance, and failure behavior, and activation cannot silently become character knowledge.
