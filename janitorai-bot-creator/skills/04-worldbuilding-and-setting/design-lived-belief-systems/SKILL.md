---

## Steps

1. Define the doctrine, stories, practices, institutions, authorities, spaces, objects, and community boundaries.
2. Separate official teaching, local custom, private practice, doubt, hypocrisy, reform, and competing interpretation.
3. Show how belief affects time, food, body, kinship, work, law, care, sex, death, art, and ordinary decisions.
4. Give {{char}} and other characters different histories, commitments, doubts, benefits, risks, and language.
5. Mark what is materially true, institutionally claimed, communally believed, privately suspected, or unknown.
6. Write rituals and conflict through concrete action, sensory detail, obligation, refusal, repair, and consequence.
7. Track who gains authority, who bears labour, who is excluded, and who can reinterpret or leave.
8. Let belief change through experience, pressure, migration, generational change, scandal, reform, or loss.
9. Keep {{user}}’s belief, offence, reverence, doubt, and action open.

## Decision rules

- Yes: Is the system one doctrine shared identically by everyone? Add variation and practice. No: continue.
- Yes: Is belief being used to diagnose or morally define a character? Show choices and context instead. No: continue.
- Yes: Are ritual, authority, and labour disconnected from daily life? Link them. No: continue.
- Yes: Does the draft decide {{user}}’s belief or response? Remove that decision. No: continue.

## Output

- Format: `Lived belief system map`
- System: list doctrine, stories, practices, authorities, spaces, and boundaries.
- Variation: list official, local, private, doubtful, and dissenting positions.
- Life: list effects on body, time, kinship, work, law, care, and death.
- Pressure: list authority, labour, exclusion, change, and scene hooks.
- Player space: list known context and open responses.

## Stop when

- No fictional belief, ritual, doctrine, or spiritual conflict is supplied: return `No belief-system update` and do not invent {{user}}’s belief.
