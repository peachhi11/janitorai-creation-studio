name: write-manipulation-and-counterplay
description: Use when writing fictional influence, manipulation, coercion, seduction, intimidation, court intrigue, gaslighting, reputation attacks, dependency, or resistance as a changing contest. Trigger phrases include “write the manipulation,” “show the counterplay,” and “make the power struggle consequence-based.”
---

## Steps

1. Define each character’s goal, leverage, information, dependency, vulnerability, boundary, and available exit.
2. State the setting, audience, time pressure, relationship history, and cost of exposure.
3. Write attempt → interpretation → counterplay → partial result → new information or leverage.
4. Give the target a plausible reading, resistance, compliance, delay, escape, alliance, or counterattack.
5. Make tactics character-specific and fallible; do not use guaranteed psychological tricks.
6. Separate persuasion, deception, pressure, threat, coercion, assault, and aftermath.
7. Carry forward reputation, fear, debt, knowledge, dependency, trust, injury, and changed options.
8. Keep operational coercion, vulnerability targeting, and objection-defeat advice out of the output.
9. Keep {{user}}’s thoughts, consent, speech, and action open.

## Decision rules

- Yes: Is the text usable as real-world manipulation or coercion instruction? Replace the tactic with fictional high-level action and consequence. No: continue.
- Yes: Is success guaranteed? Add uncertainty, resistance, and failure cost. No: continue.
- Yes: Is coercion being called seduction or mutual misunderstanding? Name the power and harm. No: continue.
- Yes: Does the draft decide {{user}}’s response? Remove that decision. No: continue.

## Output

- Format: `Fictional influence and counterplay map`
- Contest: list goals, leverage, information, dependency, vulnerability, boundary, and exit.
- Exchange: trace attempt, interpretation, counterplay, result, and new leverage.
- Consequences: list exposure, reputation, fear, debt, trust, injury, and changed options.
- Safety boundary: state that no real-world manipulation procedure is provided.

## Stop when

- No fictional power struggle or influence attempt is supplied: return `No counterplay update` and do not invent {{user}}’s response.
