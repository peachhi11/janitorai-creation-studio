## Steps

1. Define the fantasy, roles, limits, permissions, safewords or signals, aftercare needs, and what remains unknown.
2. State who owns each choice and whether the scene is consensual play, ambiguous fiction, or deliberate violation.
3. Establish the setting, privacy, tools, bodies, access needs, emotional stakes, and exit route.
4. Write negotiation → initiation → action → feedback → adjustment, pause, stop, or continuation.
5. Make commands, protocol, sensation, service, punishment, and role-play character-specific rather than instructional.
6. Show words and nonverbal feedback without treating arousal, freezing, silence, or lack of resistance as consent.
7. Include interruption, mistakes, overstimulation, aftercare, drop, debrief, or refusal when the scene calls for them.
8. For deliberate violation, show ignored boundaries and consequences without relabelling it mutual.
9. Keep {{user}}’s body, pleasure, consent, speech, action, and aftermath open.

## Decision rules

- Yes: Is the text a real-world bondage, impact, breath, medical, or consent manual? Keep it fictional and non-operational. No: continue.
- Yes: Is a safeword or signal treated as decorative? Show what it changes. No: continue.
- Yes: Is involuntary response treated as permission? Separate response from consent. No: continue.
- Yes: Does the scene force a reassuring aftermath? Preserve the requested dark or unresolved outcome. No: continue.
- Yes: Does the draft decide {{user}}’s response? Remove it. No: continue.

## Output

- Format: `Negotiated kink scene map`
- Frame: list roles, permissions, limits, signals, aftercare, and ownership.
- Scene: write negotiation, initiation, feedback, adjustment, and ending.
- Boundary: list what is consensual, ambiguous, violated, paused, or stopped.
- Aftermath: list care, drop, debrief, consequences, and open player choices.

## Stop when

- No fictional kink, power exchange, or negotiated intimacy premise is supplied: return `No kink-scene update` and do not invent {{user}}’s consent or response.
