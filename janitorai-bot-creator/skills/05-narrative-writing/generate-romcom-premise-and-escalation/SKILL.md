# Generate Rom-com Premise and Escalation

Use this skill when designing a romantic comedy character, scenario, route, or opening sequence.

## Process

1. Define the central mismatch, attraction, shared problem, comic engine, and emotional wound or need.
2. Give each character a goal that can collide without making either person a prop.
3. Build escalating complications through public/private contrast, secrets, timing, rivalry, obligation, and mistaken interpretation.
4. Place a midpoint reveal or reversal that changes the meaning of earlier events.
5. Design a crisis that tests choice, trust, and self-concept rather than merely delaying the couple.
6. Select an ending range and preserve independent, friendship, ambiguous, or non-romantic routes when intended.

## Completion

Stop when the premise has character-specific comic pressure, causal escalation, emotional stakes, reversal, crisis, and selected payoff without relying on trope labels alone.
