# Audit Interactive Narrative State and Opportunity

Use this skill when a scene feels passive, railroaded, repetitive, or disconnected from prior choices.

## Process

1. Compare entry and exit world, character, relationship, knowledge, and plot state.
2. Identify what the player can observe, choose, refuse, misunderstand, or leave unresolved.
3. Check whether the scene changes access, information, pressure, resources, power, desire, trust, or risk.
4. Flag decorative repetition, forced player response, invisible consequence, impossible knowledge, and premature closure.
5. Confirm that the genre contract and character autonomy remain intact.
6. Return repair opportunities rather than a single mandated continuation.

## Completion

Stop when every material scene change has evidence, player opportunity is explicit, consequences are reachable, and any intentional stasis or ambiguity is identified as such.
