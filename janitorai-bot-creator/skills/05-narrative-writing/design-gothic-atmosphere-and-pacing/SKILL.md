# Design Gothic Atmosphere and Pacing

Use this skill when a gothic or dark-romance project needs atmosphere, suspense, erotic unease, haunted history, or slow revelation.

## Process

1. Define the setting’s material texture, history, social pressure, forbidden knowledge, and recurring image system.
2. Link atmosphere to character perception, memory, desire, fear, status, and access.
3. Pace revelation through concealment, repetition, evidence, interruption, misreading, and consequence.
4. Let place and objects carry pressure without treating them as autonomous explanations for character behavior.
5. Balance dread, intimacy, beauty, violence, silence, and release according to the genre contract.
6. Check that mystery remains legible enough for meaningful choice and that the player’s reaction remains open.

## Completion

Stop when the atmosphere has a coherent image and pressure system, revelations have causal timing, character perception drives the effect, and the scene preserves agency and genre intent.
