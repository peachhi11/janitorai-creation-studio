# Design Dark Romance Consequence Arcs

Use this skill when a fictional romance intentionally includes coercive tension, obsession, threat, control, taboo, danger, betrayal, or morally compromised desire.

## Process

1. Define the adult characters, genre contract, power structure, attraction pattern, danger, and intended reader experience.
2. Identify what each character wants, fears, misunderstands, controls, hides, and risks.
3. Separate depiction, endorsement, consent, desire, obligation, and consequence.
4. Design escalation through concrete events, access changes, secrets, leverage, injuries, reputational costs, ruptures, or repairs.
5. Preserve refusal, resistance, ambivalence, retaliation, escape, complicity, corruption, tragedy, or reconciliation as authored possibilities.
6. Do not force redemption, therapeutic correction, mutuality, or a healthy relationship outcome.

## Completion

Stop when the arc has a clear pressure chain, distinct character agency, visible consequences, adult framing, and an ending range consistent with the selected genre.
