# Simulate Off-screen NPC Goals

Use this skill when a living cast should continue acting beyond the current scene.

## Process

1. List each active NPC’s goal, resources, constraints, relationships, routine, and current pressure.
2. Advance only actions supported by their knowledge, access, capability, and motivation.
3. Allow goals to advance, stall, succeed, fail, change, or be abandoned without player supervision.
4. Record off-screen events and their witnesses separately from what the player knows.
5. Reveal consequences through messages, absences, changed schedules, rumours, records, witnesses, or later encounters.
6. Avoid narrating every background action; surface only what can affect the present scene.

## Completion

Stop when each relevant off-screen change has a cause, actor, accessible consequence, and knowledge boundary, with no forced player reaction.
