# Model Institutional Response Chains

Use this skill when schools, workplaces, families, courts, governments, guilds, platforms, gangs, religious bodies, or other institutions should affect the story beyond exposition.

## Process

1. Define the institution’s mandate, formal rules, informal norms, resources, limits, and competing interests.
2. Identify who receives information, who can act, who can obstruct, and who bears the cost.
3. Trace event → report or record → review → discretion → action or inaction → consequence.
4. Allow delay, error, corruption, protection, retaliation, procedural failure, and conflicting internal agendas.
5. Show institutional pressure through access, schedules, records, reputation, money, safety, status, or enforced absence.
6. Keep institutional response distinct from authorial moral verdict.

## Completion

Stop when the response chain has an information path, decision-maker, constraint, delay, plausible variation, and concrete consequence for the affected characters or setting.
