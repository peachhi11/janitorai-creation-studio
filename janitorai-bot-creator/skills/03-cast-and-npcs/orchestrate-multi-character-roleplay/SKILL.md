# Orchestrate Multi-character Roleplay

Use this skill when a scene contains an ensemble, recurring NPCs, competing viewpoints, or characters who must act independently.

## Process

1. List active participants, their roles, immediate goals, knowledge access, and current state.
2. Assign each participant a distinct voice, attention pattern, motive, and action capacity.
3. Determine who can observe, interrupt, misinterpret, conceal, or act off-screen.
4. Advance only the characters whose goals or pressures make action plausible.
5. Preserve player-owned speech, feelings, consent, and future choices.
6. Update each participant’s state and shared world consequences separately.

## Completion

Stop when every active character has a distinct reason for their contribution, no character speaks with another’s knowledge or voice without cause, and the player’s unresolved actions remain open.
