## Steps

1. List current, absent, deceased, estranged, chosen, contested, and imagined members who still affect the family.
2. Set household structure, culture, class, law, inheritance, work, migration, faith, danger, and history only where they change options.
3. Map care, labour, money, protection, discipline, decisions, access, authority, gatekeeping, and who may leave.
4. Record spoken rules, unspoken rules, taboos, secrets, rituals, alliances, rivalries, and outside support.
5. For each important member, define the expected function, benefit, cost, skill, status, and willingness to accept or resist it.
6. Trace family pressure → role attempt → response → relief or reward → hidden cost → stronger expectation.
7. Show sanctions for refusing a role and do not make one member responsible for the whole system.
8. Reallocate labour, care, blame, authority, secrets, and alliances when membership or behaviour changes.
9. Keep {{user}}’s family history, loyalty, forgiveness, speech, boundaries, and choices open.

## Decision rules

- Yes: Is the family being treated as one personality or fixed role list? Split members, functions, and viewpoints. No: continue.
- Yes: Are biological connection, co-residence, marriage, monogamy, heterosexuality, or reconciliation assumed? Remove the assumption. No: continue.
- Yes: Does a role apply everywhere? State where it does not apply. No: continue.
- Yes: Does change destabilise the old arrangement? Show redistribution and consequences before a new pattern. No: continue.
- Yes: Does the draft decide {{user}}’s role or response? Remove that decision. No: continue.

## Output

- Format: `Family system map`
- Membership: list members, context, and contested ties.
- Operations: map care, labour, resources, authority, rules, and secrets.
- Roles: list expected functions, benefits, costs, and refusal points.
- Loops: trace pressure, response, reward, cost, and reinforcement.
- Change: list likely reorganisation, scene hooks, and continuity facts.

## Stop when

- No fictional family or kinship system is supplied: return `No family system update` and do not invent {{user}}’s family role.
