## Steps

1. State the example’s fictional purpose, audience, genre, viewpoint, and intended craft lesson.
2. Identify explicit or implied acts, power, consent, boundaries, consequences, and player-owned choices.
3. Separate depiction, endorsement, instruction, character belief, narrator framing, and reader interpretation.
4. Check for real-world sexual, medical, legal, safety, coercion, or objection-defeat instruction.
5. Check whether fantasy, arousal, silence, body response, or role labels are treated as consent or identity.
6. Check agency, copyright-sensitive imitation, and whether the example writes {{user}}’s response.
7. Preserve intentional darkness, taboo, compulsion, predation, or ambiguity while removing operational guidance.
8. List a safe craft-focused revision.

## Decision rules

- Yes: Could the example function as real-world instruction? Replace procedure with fictional consequence. No: continue.
- Yes: Is depiction being presented as endorsement or universal relationship advice? Separate the layers. No: continue.
- Yes: Does the example decide {{user}}’s desire, consent, body, or action? Remove it. No: continue.
- Yes: Is a living author’s style being copied? Describe craft features instead. No: continue.

## Output

- Format: `Erotic exemplar and agency audit`
- Purpose: state audience, genre, viewpoint, and intended lesson.
- Findings: list framing, instruction, consent, agency, copyright, and sovereignty issues.
- Repairs: provide craft-focused replacement wording or structure.
- Preserve: list intentional darkness, ambiguity, and open player choices.

## Stop when

- No erotic example, prompt, or scenario is supplied: return `No erotic-exemplar findings`.
