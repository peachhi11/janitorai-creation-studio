# Audit character coherence

Check whether the character’s actions make sense from what they know, want, fear, value, can do, and currently believe. Coherence does not require rationality, likability, redemption, or a single stable trait.

**Action:** Trace knowledge, state, trigger, pressure, capability, choice,
consequence, learning, and residue.

**Key point:** Coherence means traceable causation, not rationality, morality,
redemption, or sameness.

**Why:** Minimal repairs preserve canon and contradiction while closing genuine
causal gaps.

## Trace the character

For each questionable beat, record:

- canon fact, current state, and unresolved residue;
- available knowledge and what remains secret;
- trigger, appraisal, motive, value, and competing pressure;
- capability, resource, constraint, and chosen tactic;
- observable behaviour and how others interpret it;
- consequence, learning, refusal to learn, or delayed cost;
- whether the change belongs to core canon, adaptation canon, roleplay canon, or a possible invention.

## Flag weak joins

Flag a backstory detail with no present effect, a trait with no behaviour, a flaw that never costs anything, a skill that solves every problem, a sudden personality change without cause, a named NPC with no agenda, a secret disclosed without access, and a “smart” or “stupid” character whose decisions are merely author convenience. Preserve deliberate masks, lies, irrationality, and contradiction when the evidence supports them.

## Repair minimally

Suggest the smallest missing bridge: a prior cue, private belief, resource change, omitted decision, relationship consequence, knowledge boundary, or scene of failed coping. Do not rewrite canon or the player’s reaction without permission.

## Deliverable

Return a coherence table with claim, evidence, gap, impact, confidence, and minimal repair. Separate evidence, inference, and creative option.

## Narrative Intention and fictional genre logic

Treat the skill as a tool inside a chosen fictional genre, not a universal moral or psychological law. Dark, erotic, coercive, obsessive, tragic, comic, surreal, or unresolved material may be intentional. Preserve causal consequences, character specificity, truth-layer boundaries, and player sovereignty; do not turn a fictional pattern into diagnosis, a healthy-relationship lesson, or a real-world operational manual.
