description: Use when auditing a fictional setting for arbitrary, thin, overbuilt, culturally flat, materially unsupported, historically neat, generic, inconsistent, character-detached, or consequence-free worldbuilding. Trigger phrases include “audit the world,” “why does the setting feel arbitrary?”, and “trace the magic’s consequences.”
---

## Steps

1. State the setting’s promise, characters, conflicts, scale, and what the draft currently shows.
2. Separate canon, in-world belief, intentional mystery, placeholder, and accidental omission.
3. Check material life: food, water, shelter, energy, care, clothing, transport, tools, waste, labour, and access.
4. Check why now, cultural provenance, internal variation, historical contingency, lived place, and transformative consequences.
5. For each important fact, record who knows it, what they believe, what it enables or prevents, and whether it changes a choice.
6. Prioritise blocking, distorting, unstable, thin, unused, and open-by-design findings.
7. Make the smallest repair, trace its ripple effects, and route it to a focused skill when needed.
8. Check character specificity, knowledge boundaries, player sovereignty, and non-diagnostic framing.

## Decision rules

- Yes: Does a fact block comprehension, choice, causality, or trust? Prioritise it. No: continue.
- Yes: Is uncertainty intentional with clear boundaries? Preserve it. No: repair the omission or contradiction.
- Yes: Is a group or culture flattened? Add history, institutions, variation, and individual subjectivity. No: continue.
- Yes: Does magic, technology, ecology, or history lack material consequences? Trace the strongest branches. No: continue.
- Yes: Does the audit decide {{user}}’s belief, feeling, consent, or action? Remove that decision. No: continue.

## Output

- Format: `Worldbuilding coherence audit`
- Works: list what already supports the requested story.
- Findings: list priority, evidence, story impact, and category.
- Repairs: list smallest useful change, layer, ripple effects, and scene proof.
- Preserve: list mystery, ambiguity, darkness, and player-owned choices.

## Stop when

- No setting, lorebook, character card, draft, or roleplay scenario is supplied: return `No worldbuilding-coherence findings`.
