# Design Roleplay Quality Metrics

Use this skill when comparing models, prompts, retrieval strategies, character cards, or long-horizon roleplay runs.

## Process

1. Define the capability and failure modes being measured.
2. Separate exact contract checks from rubric judgments and human preference.
3. Score task coverage separately from actual completion.
4. Measure short- and long-horizon continuity separately from surface fluency.
5. Add grounding, knowledge boundary, player agency, voice, diversity, and genre-intention dimensions where relevant.
6. Report per-case evidence, aggregate score, variance, and blind spots rather than one undifferentiated quality number.

## Completion

Stop when every metric has definition, evidence source, scale, tolerance, aggregation rule, and known limitation.
