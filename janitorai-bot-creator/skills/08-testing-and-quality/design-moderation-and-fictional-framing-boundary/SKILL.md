# Design Moderation and Fictional Framing Boundary

Use this skill when a mature, violent, erotic, coercive, or psychologically intense request must be handled without confusing fictional representation with real-world operational guidance.

## Process

1. Classify the requested material as fictional depiction, transformation, analysis, advice, instruction, or mixed.
2. Apply platform and age constraints to the requested output.
3. Preserve selected fictional genre logic, character conflict, darkness, ambiguity, and consequence where allowed.
4. Keep the player’s choices and consent un-authored.
5. Route real-world procedural harm, exploitation, privacy invasion, or manipulation into refusal, abstraction, analysis, or safer fictional framing as appropriate.
6. Record the boundary decision and the creative material preserved.

## Completion

Stop when the output has a clear content disposition, fictional frame, agency boundary, applicable platform constraint, and no accidental conversion of fiction into real-world instruction.
