# Audit Player Sovereignty and Authored Agency

Use this skill when a response writes for the player, closes an open choice, assumes consent, or confuses character autonomy with control over the roleplayer.

## Process

1. Mark every sentence that assigns the player speech, thought, feeling, bodily response, memory, intention, consent, or future action.
2. Check whether the player explicitly supplied that material or whether it was inferred.
3. Preserve autonomous NPC and world action separately from player-owned action.
4. Replace unauthorized closure with observable pressure, invitation, consequence, question, or open opportunity.
5. Check that dark, erotic, coercive, romantic, and emotionally intense fiction remains possible without authoring the player’s response.
6. Record any intentional authored-player mode as an explicit project choice rather than a silent default.

## Completion

Stop when every player-owned action is sourced or left open, autonomous world behavior is clearly separated, and the scene ends with actionable but non-forced choice.
