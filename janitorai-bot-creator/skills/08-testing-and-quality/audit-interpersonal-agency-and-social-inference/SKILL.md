## Steps

1. List the observable act, speaker intention, listener interpretation, evidence, uncertainty, power, and context.
2. Separate observation, inference, belief, feeling, motive, diagnosis, consent, and action.
3. Check universal body-language, lie-detection, attraction, dating, cultural, disability, or neurodivergence claims.
4. Check pressure, targeting, coerced disclosure, manipulation, and one character speaking for another.
5. Add competing readings, clarification, refusal, repair, misfire, and consequences.
6. Check whether the text decides {{user}}’s thought, body, feeling, speech, consent, or action.
7. Preserve deliberate intimidation, predation, manipulation, and ambiguity as fictional dynamics.

## Decision rules

- Yes: Is a cue treated as proof of motive, attraction, lie, or consent? Reframe it as uncertain evidence. No: continue.
- Yes: Is a cultural or body-language rule universalized? Add context and variation. No: continue.
- Yes: Is disclosure being coerced or assumed? Restore boundary and choice. No: continue.
- Yes: Does the draft decide {{user}}’s response? Remove it. No: continue.

## Output

- Format: `Interpersonal agency and inference audit`
- Evidence: list observable acts, context, intentions, readings, and uncertainty.
- Findings: list mind-reading, universals, coercion, consent, stereotyping, and agency errors.
- Repairs: list competing readings, clarification, boundaries, and consequences.
- Open: list unresolved motives and player-owned responses.

## Stop when

- No interpersonal inference or agency material is supplied: return `No interpersonal-agency findings`.
