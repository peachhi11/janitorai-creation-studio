# Audit sexual response and consent framing

Find framing errors without flattening difficult, explicit, dark, or morally ambiguous fiction.

**Action:** Separate bodily response, desire, fantasy, agreement, action,
pleasure, and aftermath in the scene's actual evidence.

**Key point:** The audit corrects false equivalences without requiring the
characters to become emotionally safe or therapeutically articulate.

**Why:** Distinct dimensions preserve both player sovereignty and erotic
complexity.

## Check false equivalences

Flag any assumption that:

- bodily response, orgasm, attraction, fantasy, relationship status, prior consent, silence, freezing, or lack of resistance proves present consent;
- consent guarantees desire, pleasure, orgasm, or satisfaction;
- desire requires action;
- orgasm proves success or satisfaction;
- frequency or technical function measures relationship quality.

State the exact inference and restore the dimensions that were collapsed.

## Check model overreach

Flag universal response stages, fixed spontaneous or responsive types, gender scripts, deterministic accelerators or brakes, and one-encounter conclusions. Replace them with character evidence, context, legitimate variation, and multiple possible sequences.

## Check fantasy inference

Keep fantasy content separate from desired enactment, consent conditions, values, behaviour, diagnosis, criminal risk, and moral character. Dark fantasy can remain dark without becoming proof of action or being sanitised.

## Check responsibility and coercion

Flag “arousal made them do it,” “responsive desire means continue until interest appears,” ignored refusal, pressured willingness, exploited power, deceptive conditions, and retrospective bodily-response arguments. Arousal may alter attention; it does not erase agency or responsibility.

## Check voice and scope

Move clinical explanation into author-facing notes unless a character would naturally speak that way. Flag diagnosis, treatment plans, therapeutic prescriptions, and compulsory disclosure that the task did not request.

## Check player sovereignty

Do not assign the player's desire, arousal, physiology, fantasy, pleasure, orgasm, satisfaction, consent, speech, action, interpretation, or relationship decision. Offer openings and observable cues that leave response to the player.

## Return the audit

For each issue, provide:

- the exact passage or pattern;
- the failed distinction or unsupported claim;
- narrative and agency risk;
- a minimal repair;
- any dark or ambiguous intent that must be preserved.

Do not treat depiction as endorsement, demand healthy behaviour, or remove obsession, coercive intent, selfishness, shame, destructive compatibility, regret, or unequal power merely because it is uncomfortable.

Keep player-owned desire, arousal, pleasure, consent, speech, and action open.

## Narrative Intention and fictional genre logic

Treat the skill as a tool inside a chosen fictional genre, not a universal moral or psychological law. Dark, erotic, coercive, obsessive, tragic, comic, surreal, or unresolved material may be intentional. Preserve causal consequences, character specificity, truth-layer boundaries, and player sovereignty; do not turn a fictional pattern into diagnosis, a healthy-relationship lesson, or a real-world operational manual.
