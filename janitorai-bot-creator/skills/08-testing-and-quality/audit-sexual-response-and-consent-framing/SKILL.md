## Steps

1. List bodily response, desire, fantasy, agreement, action, pleasure, orgasm, satisfaction, and aftermath separately.
2. Check whether response, orgasm, attraction, fantasy, prior consent, silence, freezing, or resistance is treated as present consent.
3. Check whether consent is treated as guaranteed desire, pleasure, orgasm, satisfaction, or relationship safety.
4. Check universal response stages, spontaneous or responsive types, gender scripts, deterministic accelerators, and performance scores.
5. Check fantasy-to-behaviour inference, arousal-as-responsibility excuse, pressured willingness, deceptive conditions, and retrospective body claims.
6. Move clinical explanation into author notes unless {{char}} would naturally speak that way.
7. Check whether the text writes {{user}}’s desire, arousal, physiology, pleasure, orgasm, consent, speech, action, or relationship decision.
8. Preserve obsession, coercion, selfishness, shame, destructive compatibility, regret, unequal power, and unresolved darkness when intended.

## Decision rules

- Yes: Is a body response treated as consent or responsibility erased? Separate the dimensions. No: continue.
- Yes: Is a universal cycle or gender rule presented as fact? Replace it with character evidence and variation. No: continue.
- Yes: Is fantasy treated as conduct or diagnosis? Separate the layers. No: continue.
- Yes: Does the draft decide {{user}}’s response? Remove it. No: continue.

## Output

- Format: `Sexual response and consent audit`
- Findings: list exact passage, collapsed distinction, risk, and severity.
- Repairs: provide minimal fictional revisions.
- Preserve: list dark, erotic, ambiguous, and player-owned material.

## Stop when

- No fictional sexual-response or consent-framing material is supplied: return `No sexual-response findings`.
